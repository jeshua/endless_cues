# Changelog

What changed in each version of Endless Cues, newest first. Versions are numbered as `docs/RELEASING.md` says.
Until 1.0, anything may change between versions; a show file saved by an older version always opens in a newer one.

`docs/qlab-tracker.md` is the detailed list of what is done, what is partly done, and what has not yet met the
equipment it is for. This file is the short version, for someone deciding whether to update.

## What is in it
The whole app, as it is now (what changed in each version follows).

- **Show control in the manner of QLab**, free and open source: a cue list with one GO, a playhead, groups (timeline, start first and enter, playlists), sections, carts, hotkeys and triggers, Show and Edit modes, double-GO protection, Panic, Run from here, Check the show, and a show log. Nothing is kept until you save; every save is a version you can compare and restore, and timed backups keep unsaved work.
- **Sound**: Audio, Fade, Mic, Mix and Jump cues on named buses, with loops and loop regions, markers, tempo and bar lines, lines drawn over a sound (volume, pan, any effect setting), a rack of effects per cue (filters, eq, gate, compressor, drive, pitch, chorus, delay, reverb…), fade shapes, ducking by kind (off until switched on), a matrix of outputs with trims, a monitor feed and recording. The Playing strip steers a running track: hold a loop, skip or fade on the bar.
- **The native audio engine** (experimental, off until chosen in Settings ▸ Audio): `ec-audio`, in Rust, opens the output device itself; anything it cannot do yet stays with Web Audio.
- **Video, Text and Camera cues** on a stage window on a second display, with geometry that fades.
- **Lighting**: Light cues with levels by command line, a patch, groups and fixtures, sent as Art-Net or sACN; or cues fired on an ETC Eos or grandMA3 desk.
- **Network and devices**: OSC and MIDI cues (with faded values), MIDI files, MSC in and out, MTC and LTC in and out, timecode triggers, raw network messages, OSC control from remotes built for QLab (passcodes with levels), a web remote for a phone, show feedback, and a main and backup link.
- **The rig**: the show's equipment on one page, each device with named commands, asked whether it is there; Device cues; a prop kit with firmware for ESP32 boards; a show from another computer opens with its network features off until turned on.
- **The assistant** (off until set up): reads the show, proposes changes as one reviewable edit, and shows where things are in the window. It works through Claude Code or Codex, your own key (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, any OpenAI-compatible service, Ollama), or an Endless Cues account. It never edits or plays a show by itself.
- **QLab import**: QLab 4 and 5 workspaces open as shows, with a report of what did not come across.
- **macOS, Windows and Linux**; signed builds for the Mac, updates from inside the app, Report a Problem from Help.

## Unreleased
- **Find loops.** On an Audio cue's Time & Loops tab, **Find loops** listens to the sound and adds up to four loop regions that go round cleanly, on its bar lines when it has a tempo, spread over the track. Undo takes them away. It runs only when asked.
- **Drag a stretch on the Playing strip to loop it.** While a sound plays, drag across its waveform under the list: that stretch goes round at once (its ends on the beats; ⇧ for exactly where you let go) until you let go. In Edit mode, **Keep** saves it as one of the sound's loop regions.
- **Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later** (it was the AGPL). The same freedoms; running a changed copy as a service no longer obliges anyone to publish their changes. Versions already published stay under the AGPL.

## 0.3.0-alpha.6 · 2026-11-18
- **QLab import, checked against QLab itself.** Fifty-three workspaces (QLab 4.0 to 5.6) were imported and compared, field by field, with what QLab 5.6.3 says about them; seventeen kinds of thing that came across wrong now come across. Text cues bring their words, font, size, colour and alignment; Timecode cues their start, frame rate and LTC output; wall-clock and timecode triggers, ducking, the second trigger, auto-load and "fade and stop others" (with which cues it stops) are kept; a MIDI trigger keeps the value it asked for (a controller at one value only); Reset and Target cues are cues again; a Fade that moves a picture keeps where it moves it; MSC commands with no words here go as their bytes; QLab's colours all have a match. The import report says less that is not so: no "silent on outputs of its own" for a cue that is not, and a MIDI cue with no message says what it held.
- **Played against QLab.** A mono sound on QLab's default crosspoints plays on the left, as in QLab (it was in the middle); a QLab cue's second trigger "does nothing" comes across; Fade cues keep QLab's curves, with two new fade shapes; a cue that ducks the others lands on its time in both audio engines.
- **The native audio engine** (`ec-audio`, experimental, off until chosen in Settings ▸ Audio ▸ Audio engine): the same player as the audio window, opening the output device itself, tested without an interface (capture, listening, counts of underruns).
- **The Live view is gone.** The cue list's own tools for steering a running track (the Playing strip, holds, skips and fades on the bar) are unchanged.
- **Only today's show format is read.** A show saved by an older version still opens. What is gone: the older single pair of ducking (if you had switched it on, set up Ducking by kind in Settings ▸ Audio again); OSC control's one older passcode is read as a passcode that may do everything, as it was. A show you had opened before may ask once to turn on its network features.
- Under the bonnet: every source file is TypeScript, checked strictly.

## 0.3.0-alpha.5 · 2026-11-17
- **No more hover tips in the inspector.** The few fields whose name needs explaining (Flagged, Armed, Continue, the waits, Fade others, Auto-load, Bus, Outputs, End at…) have a small ⓘ beside the name: click it to read one line, click anywhere else to put it away.
- **An audio cue's Audio tab opens on its waveform**, with the Volume line (and the menu of its other lines) and the markers, edited right there. Time & Loops keeps the typed-in times, the tempo and the loop regions.
- **Remove all markers**: beside the markers on Time & Loops, and in the waveform's right-click menu. One undo puts them back.
- **A newer version is easy to see.** The title bar's update line is a pill in the accent, with an arrow, and Download (later Restart now) is the main button. Still never in Show mode or while anything plays.
- **Safer with a show or a packet from someone else.** No window of the app can be sent to another page (a link, a file dropped on it): each page talks to the show, so it stays the app's. Only web and mail addresses are handed to the system. A web remote with no key of its own gets one that cannot be worked out from the show's id (which OSC control tells anyone who asks); its address changes once. A packet that is not OSC on the lighting desk's port is dropped. A show file's settings cannot carry an object's prototype.
- **The assistant's "apply at once" never opens a door.** A change that switches a module on or off, or touches the network patches or the devices, always waits for review.
- **A show from another computer opens with its network features off.** A show not made, saved or turned on on this computer starts no module (OSC control, the web remote, the lighting desk…), opens no port, connects to nothing, sends nothing, asks no device whether it is there, and starts no cue by a clock, OSC, MIDI or timecode. A sound on another computer's share (`\\host\share`) is not opened. One line in the title bar says so, with **Turn on**; that is remembered for the show on this computer. Your recent shows count as this computer's, so nothing that already worked is held back.
- **OSC control never lets a sender with no passcode edit.** With no passcode, anyone may view and run the show (GO, stop, panic, load, the playhead) but not change it: deleting, saving, new cues and setting a cue's properties take a passcode with edit access. Over UDP, only a sender that has sent `/connect` is answered (and no address more than 32 KB a second), so a forged address cannot be sent the show's cue lists. Remotes built for QLab connect first and are answered on 53001 as before; a GO box that only sends `/go` still works.
- **Codex as the assistant can read files on this computer.** Its command-line tool has no switch to take away its own shell; it runs in a read-only sandbox, so it can read and run commands that only read, never change anything. Settings ▸ Assistant now says so.

## 0.3.0-alpha.4 · 2026-11-17
- **The assistant knows the app, and can show you.** Ask it where something is or what a key does ("where do I change a cue's colour?", "what's the key for Panic?") and it answers from a guide to the window and its keys, and rings the place on the screen for a few seconds: an inspector tab is brought forward, a cue selected, Settings opened at the pane. Its reply has a **Show me** to do it again. Never in Show mode; it changes nothing. The built-in helper answers the common ones too, with no model.
- **Lines over a sound (automation)**: Time & Loops draws the Volume line all the time, with no button to open it. Click the line to add a point, drag points (time and level together; they snap lightly to markers and beats, Shift for fine), ⌘-drag a box round several to move or delete them together, ⌥-drag a stretch to bend it into a curve, double-click a point to remove it. A small menu above the waveform picks the line: Volume, Pan, or any setting of the cue's effects that can glide ("Low-pass · Cutoff", "Reverb · Mix"); the others are drawn faint behind. A knob with a line shows ∿, and turning it moves the whole line. Lines follow the sound's place in its file, so they go round with a loop, and survive a skip, a pause, a rate, Load to Time and Run from here. A line edited while the sound plays is heard at once. Old envelopes play exactly as they did.
- **Pan** on Audio and Mic cues (−100 left … 100 right, equal power). A QLab cue panned by its crosspoints between outputs 1 and 2 imports as a pan.
- **Markers and loop regions are dragged on the waveform**: markers have a strip of their own above it (drag to move, double-click to rename, ⌥-click to remove, drag on the strip or right-click to add one); a loop region's edges drag, and its middle moves it whole.
- **< and > fold and unfold every group, and leave the sections alone.** Sections fold together from View ▸ Fold All Sections, a section's right-click, or ⌥-click on a section's caret; View ▸ Unfold Everything opens both.
- **A calmer window.** Six cue colours and "…" for any colour at all (a colour wheel; the list's right-click has Colour ▸ too). The add-cue bar shows Group, Audio, Fade, Stop and Memo; **+** lists the rest, and typing finds one (Enter adds it). Save says how many changes are waiting ("Save · 28"). Versions, the light dashboard and the monitor are in a **⋯** menu (their keys are unchanged). Panic is large; Pause all and Fade all are small, and Reset is there only in Edit. With nothing playing the side panel says so in one line; its Network messages show only once you ask (⋯ ▸ Network in the Side Panel). A list column nobody uses (Target, Pre-wait, Duration, Post-wait) is left out until a cue needs it, and stays put through Show mode. The mixer's knobs lose their − and +: drag, scroll, double-click to type, ⌥-click for 0 dB, or Tab to one and use the arrows. Pre-wait and Post-wait wait under More until they are set (Post-wait also shows with auto-continue). The assistant's face is small, beside its words.
- **Report, when something goes wrong.** Report is always in the status bar. When the app hits an error (a sound that cannot play, an output gone, a window that had to be made again), the status bar offers "Something went wrong. Report it?"; the report then carries that error and the recent error lines, each of which you can untick. In Show mode it waits as a small dot and says itself back in Edit.
- **Chains you can see**: cues linked by auto-continue or auto-follow have a thin line down the left of the list (solid: together; dotted: one after the other), and the masthead says "GO · 3 cues" when the next GO sets off more than one. Delete or cut a cue from the middle of a chain and the list shows what the cue before it now links to, and says so.
- **Stop one sound from its row**: every playing row has its own ■. Click: it fades out over the panic time. Click again, or ⌥-click: it stops dead. It is there in Show mode too, larger, and never moves the playhead.
- **Playing but silent?** A playing sound's row has a small level meter; hover it to see where the sound goes. A sound whose output is not connected says "no output" in red.
- **Find takes you there**: each match is unfolded out of its section or group, brought to the middle of the list, selected and flashed. The playhead stays put.
- **Zoom out the cue list**: pinch, ⌘− / ⌘=, or the − in the list's header, down to the whole show in the window (rows become bars in their cue's colour; headings, the cue standing by and running cues stay visible). Click anywhere there to zoom back in. Remembered for each show on this computer.
- **A fade to silence stops what it faded.** A Fade cue whose level goes to −∞ (the show's floor) now stops its target when it is done, unless its "Stop target when done" is switched off; QLab leaves the sound playing, silent, unless asked. Any other fade leaves it playing, as before. A saved show's fades to silence that never set the switch now stop their targets; an imported QLab workspace keeps QLab's own setting on every fade.
- **Check the show** (Tools ▸ Check the Show, ⇧⌘F, or Check in the footer; also on the Rig page): one list of what would go wrong, found without playing anything. Missing files, files that cannot be played here, targets that are gone, sounds sent to outputs this device does not have, cues that can never play, cues that start each other for ever, triggers that answer to the same message, and equipment the Rig page says is not answering. A click goes to the cue.
- **Run from here** (right-click a cue ▸ Run from Here…, or Tools ▸ Run from the Selected Cue…): a rehearsal at that cue, with what would be going on there had the show run: loops and held sections, sounds the show stops later, at the levels the fades before left them; the last command each device, address and mixer bus was sent. It says what it will start first ("Starts 3 sounds and sets 2 devices"). Edit mode only.
- **Double-GO protection covers every GO**, from the button, Space, a remote, OSC, MIDI or MSC, and a cue's own trigger repeating: the same key, message, cart button or remote start for the same cue within the time (Settings ▸ General, 0.3 s) is ignored. A trigger is not a GO, so it never locks GO.
- **The show log**: every GO and what it started, each cue starting, stopping and fading and what set it off, the GOs ignored, missing files and other problems, mode changes and saves, in the show's `logs/` folder (one file a sitting, as words and as a table; the newest 20 kept). View ▸ Show Log (⌥⌘L) shows it as it happens, by kind, searchable, with Copy and Reveal. Report a Problem… can include its last lines, with every cue's and file's name taken out (off unless ticked).
- **Sound design is a rack**: the tab shows only the effects a cue uses, as devices in a row in the order they play, each with a power button, small knobs (drag, scroll, double-click for the default, double-click the value to type) and × to remove it. **+** adds an effect from a short list. A filter and the EQ draw their curve. Presets, Bypass and Tail sit in the tab's header. Shows are unchanged: the same `chain` and `eq` are read and written. A knob turned on a device that is off now leaves it off (before, typing a value switched the tool on).

## 0.3.0-alpha.2 · 2026-11-16
- **Updates**: once a day, in Edit mode with nothing playing, the app asks downloads.endlesscues.com whether there is a newer version (one request, with nothing about you or your show in it). If there is, one line in the title bar says so: Download, or Not now. It installs when you quit, or on Restart now. Nothing happens in Show mode. ## 0.3.0-alpha.1 · 2026-11-16
- **Sections**: a heading in the cue list ("Act two", "Scene 3") that never plays. GO, continues and auto-follow pass straight over it; it holds the cues under it up to the next one. Add one from More ▸ Section, or right-click ▸ Add Section Above (Section from Selection with several selected). Click a heading to fold it; ⌥-click folds every one; ← → fold the selected section or group; `<` `>` and View ▸ Fold All / Unfold All do all of them. A folded heading shows the cue standing by in it, and in Show mode the section you are in opens by itself. The section names sit in a strip above the list: click one to go there. What is folded is remembered for each show on this computer, not saved in the show. Jump to cue takes a section's name. Cues made from a folder now get a section per scene or sub-folder instead of a group.

- The **Endless Cues assistant**: the first row of "Who should answer?". Sign in from the app with a code confirmed in the browser; no key, nothing to install. Free during the beta: 100 asks a month, then a smaller model, then a stop until the 1st. Settings ▸ Assistant shows the month's asks and signs out. Nothing contacts the service until it is chosen.
- **Report a problem** (Help ▸ Report a Problem…, or the link in Settings ▸ About): a bug, an idea or anything else, sent to endlesscues.com with no account. A picture of the window goes with it unless you untick it (you see it before it is sent), and the show's name only if you tick it. The form says what else is sent (the app's version, your system, what was on screen, recent error messages with your name and paths taken out) and never sent (show files, sounds, keys), and shows it exactly if you ask. With no connection the report is kept on this computer and sent when the app next opens or when you press Send again. Nothing is sent unless you press Send: no telemetry, no crash reports.
- The assistant has a face: **QIRX-G**, drawn flat with its pentagon eye lit,, a robot puppet from Endless Forms, in the side panel beside "Ask the assistant". It is unlit when the assistant is off or the app is in Show mode, lit low when it is ready, brightens and dims while it thinks, and has a yellow dot and a count beside it when a change is waiting. Right-click it to hide it.
- The assistant's drawer says far less: four short rows for who should answer, one line and one button on an empty show. Choosing an answerer that cannot answer (Claude Code not found, a key or model name missing) now says why instead of doing nothing.
- Equipment is part of the show. A **device** is a lighting desk, a mixer, a projector or a prop: its address, its commands by name, and a way of telling whether it is there. A **Device cue** sends one of its commands. Network patches and old shows are unchanged, and a patch becomes a device in one click.
- The **Rig** page (⇧⌘R, always in the title bar): everything the show depends on, whether each part is ready, and what to do about each part that is not; Try for every command; Look for props; a checklist of your own. A device that stops answering turns its row red and says so once.
- The assistant builds the show with you: **Start from a Folder…** makes a folder of sounds the show, and the assistant's drawer says what is in it and offers the next step as a button. It can add devices and Device cues, ask a device whether it is there (never send it a command), answer "is everything ready?", and write files for you to review.
- A **built-in helper** answers when no model is set up: no internet, a short list of jobs (cues from a folder, a device, a cue for a command, readiness, firmware, the hand-over), and it says plainly what it does not do.
- A **prop kit**: firmware for a light or a switch on an ESP32, written into the show folder from a form. It compiles; it has never been run on a board.
- **Hand the show over**: a guide in the show folder (`AGENTS.md`) so that Claude Code or Codex can do what the app does not, through the app's own tools.
- `ec check`, `ec devices`, `ec try`, `ec fake-prop` (a stand-in prop, to try all of this with no hardware) and `ec sounds`. `docs/try-the-assistant.md` is a ten-minute walk through it.
- Fixed: in the assistant's drawer a change waiting for review could be squeezed until its Accept button was out of reach. The line Settings gives for coding agents works from an installed app.
- Not yet tried on equipment: any device but the stand-in prop, the kit's firmware on a board, Look for props on a real network.
- The Playing strip under the cue list has the tools for a show that leaves the script: go round the next bars (or seconds) from here until you let go, bar lines and a bar count on the playing waveform, a button for each marker, "+ here", and skips and crossfades that land on the bar. A found tempo is corrected on Time & Loops. None of it needs the Live view. Checked by timing, not yet heard.
- The start screen and Settings ▸ About show the mark; an `.ecshow` show has a document icon.
- Show mode also refuses tidying the show's sounds and keeping a marker.

## 0.2.0-alpha.1 · 2026-11-15
- The first version offered as a download.
- Sound: each bus goes to a pair of the interface's outputs; a cue can have outputs of its own, channel by channel; files of more than two channels; a reverb and a delay as sends; ducking of one bus under another; Mic cues; fade shapes and a level envelope drawn on the waveform; rate, with or without keeping pitch; level limits; recording of the main outputs; AIFF and CAF files now play.
- Loops: a loop region goes round a set number of times, or until it is let go (L, or a Devamp), and a click track goes round with its track; a play count; regions drawn on the waveform.
- Cues: MIDI, MIDI File, Timecode (MTC and LTC), Video, Text, Camera, Light (levels held and faded over Art-Net or sACN), Script, Reset and Target cues; groups that play as a playlist, start their first cue, or start one at random; cue carts; fade and stop others on any cue; a second trigger; ducking the others while a cue runs.
- Triggers on any cue: a hotkey, MIDI, OSC, a time of day, timecode. A table of every trigger in the show.
- Editing: cut, copy and paste (between shows too), Paste Cue Properties, Find, a right-click menu, cue templates, drag a cue onto a cue to aim at it, twenty colours, row sizes, notes edited where they are read, every single key changeable in Settings.
- Running: each cue list keeps its own playhead and runs on when another is shown; Hold Back keeps OSC, MIDI, timecode and DMX from going out in rehearsal; timed backups; a playback log.
- The Live view: a mixing desk over the same show, with tempo found from the sound (sections for a track that changes tempo), launch on the beat or the bar, warp to the desk's tempo, and keys and MIDI controls mapped to it.
- QLab: QLab 4 workspaces open as well as QLab 5; MIDI cues, playlists, slices, carts, hotkeys, level matrices and play counts come across; a sound whose name changed only in its punctuation is found.
- Remote control: far more of QLab's OSC vocabulary, over TCP as well as UDP, with access levels; faders and Load to Time on the web remote; a second computer can follow the first.
- The assistant sees and can change everything above, never undoes an edit made while it was working, and says what an edit would break.
- Fixed: drop-down menus flickered and stuck; a fade out on a cue with an End at was never applied; keys typed just after an edit box opened could be lost; the app held a gigabyte or more after long tracks.
- Not yet tried on the equipment it is for: output routing on a multi-output interface, MIDI and timecode on any device, DMX on any fixture, video on a projector, a Mic cue on a microphone. Not yet run on Windows or Linux.
