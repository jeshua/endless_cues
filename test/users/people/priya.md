# Priya, sound designer on a play

## Who

Sound designer and operator for small theatres: 60 to 150 seats, straight plays, a few a year. Has built every show in QLab for nine years and knows it by hand: the keys, the inspector tabs, where every setting is. Designs on a MacBook; the theatre's booth computer is whatever the theatre has.

Careful and fast. Does not read manuals for things that look familiar; presses the QLab key and sees what happens. Reads a manual when stuck.

## The show

*The Glass Orchard*, a two-act play in a 99-seat room. Fifteen sound cues: preshow music, a rain bed that runs for most of act 1, thunder that the rain dips under, a phone that rings until an actor picks it up, a door slam, an underscore, interval music, a storm, a blackout, bows. The lighting desk takes a MIDI Show Control GO from one cue.

The show is built and in tech. The QLab workspace is on the desk with its audio folder beside it.

## Why she is here

The theatre's booth computer is a Windows PC this season, and the theatre will not buy a Mac for one show. She wants to know whether she can bring the show across and run it tonight's tech from Endless Cues, and how much of her evening it will cost.

## On the desk

- `The Glass Orchard/The Glass Orchard.qlab5` and `The Glass Orchard/audio/`
- `new from the composer/letter v2.wav`
- `tech notes.txt`: this afternoon's notes

## Tasks

1. **bring** Open the QLab workspace in Endless Cues. Find out exactly what came across and what did not, without checking every cue by hand.
2. **verify** Decide whether you trust the import: check that the rain bed loops at its level, that cue 3 dips the rain under the thunder and brings it back, and that the phone stops when cue 5 fires. Run those cues and listen.
3. **repair** For each thing that did not come across (the preshow playlist, the fade of the preshow, the MSC cue, the act 2 group, the blackout), make it work some other way, or decide it cannot be done.
4. **notes** Do the tech notes in `tech notes.txt`, all six.
5. **save** Save the show into a folder on the desk so it can be carried to the booth computer. Check the sound files went with it.
6. **run** Go into the mode you would run a performance in and run the first eight cues as if it were the show. Recover from one mistake: press GO one cue too early, and get back to where you should be without the audience noticing more than they must.
7. **undo** You regret the 6.5 cue. Get the show back to how it was before you added it, without losing the other notes.

## What she judges it on

- Does the import tell the truth, and can she act on what it says?
- Do her hands still work: do the QLab keys do what they do in QLab?
- Can she tell at a glance what is playing, at what level, and what GO will do next?
- Would she sit in front of a paying audience with it tonight?
