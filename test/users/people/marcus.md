# Marcus, playback operator on a musical

## Who

Keyboard player and playback operator for regional and touring musicals. Runs tracks and click from QLab beside a pit of six. Works for the music director, not the sound designer: the tracks have to start with the downbeat, vamp for as long as the stage needs, and leave the vamp in time. Uses an eight-output audio interface: tracks to the desk on 1 and 2, click to the band's in-ears on 3 and 4.

Knows the parts of QLab a musical needs very well (slices, devamp, load to time, output routing) and almost nothing else. Impatient with anything that is not about the music.

## The show

*Songbook*, a small musical. Three numbers are on tracks: the Opening (with a vamp), a Ballad, and the Finale (with a counted vamp and a bows vamp). Each number is a group of a track and a click. There is a MIDI patch change to a keyboard, a Mic cue for a reverb return, and timecode to the lighting desk.

The QLab workspace is on the desk with its audio folder beside it.

## Why he is here

The production is buying a second playback computer as a backup and the budget is gone. He has been asked whether the backup can run something free.

## On the desk

- `Songbook/Songbook.qlab5` and `Songbook/audio/`
- `from the MD.txt`: what the music director needs

## Tasks

1. **bring** Open the QLab workspace. Read what did and did not come across.
2. **click** Find out where the click will come out. The music director's rule is that it never reaches the house. Decide whether this app can keep that rule.
3. **vamp** Make the Opening vamp from 0:16 to 0:24 for as long as needed, and leave the vamp cleanly at the end of a pass when you say so. Run it: go round at least twice, then leave.
4. **counted** The Finale has a section that plays four times and then goes on by itself. Find out whether that can be done.
5. **rehearse** Start the Ballad from the second verse (0:25 in), as you would twenty times in a rehearsal. Find the fastest way to do it again.
6. **renumber** Renumber the Finale's cues 300, 301, 302…
7. **levels** The Ballad track is 3 dB too loud against the band. Take it down while it is playing, and make sure that is how it plays tomorrow.
8. **panic** Something is wrong mid-number. Stop all sound now. Then get back to the top of that number, ready to go again.

## What he judges it on

- Can it vamp and leave a vamp in time? If not, nothing else matters.
- Can the click be kept out of the house?
- How many presses to start from the middle of a number?
- Does it say plainly what it cannot do, or does he find out during a run?
